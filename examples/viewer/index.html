    PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), Excel (.xlsx), CSV and TSV, Visio (.vsdx, .vdx), Windows metafiles (.emf, .wmf) or bdf. The file is converted to bdf and drawn inside your browser, by the Go converters built as WebAssembly: it is not uploaded anywhere.

    Password-protected files open with their password. A PDF shows its pages as they are converted, the ones in view first. Office documents are laid out with the free fonts published with this page (fetched when a document uses them), which stand in for the fonts the document names.
